Word Pop Kids – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Word Pop Kids is a spelling game for children, made by [YOUR NAME OR LLC NAME]. This policy explains what happens to information in the game.

We do not collect any information

Word Pop Kids does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not use the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things only on the phone or tablet it is played on, so it can remember them next time:

  • Stars earned and game settings (hints, sounds, calm mode, phonics).
  • The progress report: which words were spelled, when, and which letters were mixed up.
  • An optional child's name, if a parent types one into the progress report.

This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app, or tapping "Erase all progress" in the progress report, removes it.

Speech

Letters and words are read aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into the device. The game sends only the letter or word being spoken to that voice. It does not record or listen to anyone.

Children

Word Pop Kids is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.
